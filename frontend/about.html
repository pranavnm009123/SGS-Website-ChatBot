Our Story

We are SGS Technologies

Founded in 2010, SGS Technologies has grown from a two-person startup into a global technology consultancy serving clients across 30 countries.

Our mission is simple: help organizations harness the power of technology to do more, move faster, and create lasting impact.

We believe great software starts with great people. That's why we invest heavily in our team — attracting curious, driven engineers, designers, and strategists who bring creative thinking to every engagement.
