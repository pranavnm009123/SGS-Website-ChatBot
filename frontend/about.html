About SGS Technologie

An experienced software development company headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, delivering solutions beyond expectations since 2003.

Our aim is simple: develop technological solutions that benefit lives.

Being a client-centric company, our approaches are kept simple, transparent, and uniquely customized to meet each client's requirements. We commenced in 2003 with the core approach of delivering solutions even beyond expectations.

From offering legacy modernization to partnering with industry leaders, we deliver in-house products that bring resolution, clarity, and control to your operations.

  • Client-centric approach with transparent communication
  • Full-cycle software development and modernization
  • Dedicated onshore and offshore delivery teams
  • Proven track record with government and enterprise clients
