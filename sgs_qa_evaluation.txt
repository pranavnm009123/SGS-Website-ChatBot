1. [EASY] What is the Recovery Time Objective (RTO) for mission-critical systems at SGS?
   - ANSWER: 1 hour.
   - SOURCE: SGS_Disaster_Recovery_Plan.pdf, Section 1.0

2. [EASY] How many days of PTO do SGS employees receive?
   - ANSWER: 20 days PTO plus 10 company holidays.
   - SOURCE: SGS_Employee_Excellence_Handbook.pdf, Section 2.0

3. [MEDIUM] What is the minimum number of competitive bids required for vendor engagements exceeding $25,000 annually?
   - ANSWER: A minimum of three competitive bids.
   - SOURCE: SGS_Vendor_Compliance_Manual.pdf, Section 2.0

4. [MEDIUM] What is the response time requirement for a SEV-1 (Critical) security incident?
   - ANSWER: 15 minutes, with mandatory executive notification.
   - SOURCE: SGS_Information_Security_Protocol.pdf, Section 5.0

5. [EASY] What percentage of sprint capacity must engineering teams allocate to technical debt reduction?
   - ANSWER: A minimum of 20% (approximately 2 days per developer per sprint).
   - SOURCE: SGS_Technical_Debt_Guidelines.pdf, Section 2.0

================================================================================

6. [MEDIUM] Both the Cloud Architecture Standards and Disaster Recovery Plan discuss system availability. What SLO availability target does the Cloud Architecture doc set, and what is the Tier 1 RTO in the DR plan?
   - ANSWER: 99.95% availability (Cloud Architecture, Section 5.0) and 1-hour RTO (Disaster Recovery, Section 1.0).
   - SOURCE: SGS_Cloud_Architecture_Standards.pdf (Section 5.0) + SGS_Disaster_Recovery_Plan.pdf (Section 1.0)

7. [HARD] Compare the vendor security assessment process with the information security vulnerability management program. How do their remediation timelines differ?
   - ANSWER: Vendor VSQ failures allow up to 60 days for remediation (Vendor Manual, Section 3.0). Critical vulnerabilities (CVSS 9.0+) must be patched within 48 hours (InfoSec Protocol, Section 3.0).
   - SOURCE: SGS_Vendor_Compliance_Manual.pdf (Section 3.0) + SGS_Information_Security_Protocol.pdf (Section 3.0)

8. [MEDIUM] What code review standard is required before merging, and what testing coverage threshold must backend services meet?
   - ANSWER: At least two reviewer approvals including one Senior Engineer (Quality Audit, Section 4.0) and 80% unit test coverage for backend services (Quality Audit, Section 1.0).
   - SOURCE: SGS_Quality_Audit_Framework.pdf (Sections 1.0 and 4.0)

9. [HARD] How does the data retention schedule in the Data Sovereignty Policy interact with the vendor offboarding data return timeline in the Vendor Compliance Manual?
   - ANSWER: Active customer data is retained for contract duration plus 12 months (Data Sovereignty, Section 4.0), while vendors must return or destroy all SGS data within 14 days of termination (Vendor Manual, Section 5.0).
   - SOURCE: SGS_Data_Sovereignty_Policy.pdf (Section 4.0) + SGS_Vendor_Compliance_Manual.pdf (Section 5.0)

10. [MEDIUM] What are the Innovation Lab budget allocation and the total projected revenue for FY2026?
    - ANSWER: The Innovation Lab receives 15% of R&D budget (~$4.2 million), and total projected revenue is $38.7 million.
    - SOURCE: SGS_Strategic_Roadmap_2026.pdf (Sections 3.0 and 5.0)

20. [MEDIUM] The About page values list cites a proven track record with government and enterprise clients. How many new geographic markets is SGS targeting in 2026 per the Strategic Roadmap?

    - SOURCE: Website (about.html, values list) + SGS_Strategic_Roadmap_2026.pdf (Section 1.0)

21. [HARD] Across the Disaster Recovery Plan, Cloud Architecture Standards, and Information Security Protocol, what is the full picture of how SGS handles a production system outage?
    - ANSWER: Active-active failover across two AWS regions with automated Route 53 health checks (DR Plan, Section 2.0). Services auto-scale via Kubernetes HPA with 3-50 replicas (Cloud Arch, Section 2.0). SEV-1 incidents require 15-minute response with executive notification (InfoSec, Section 5.0).
    - SOURCE: SGS_Disaster_Recovery_Plan.pdf (Section 2.0) + SGS_Cloud_Architecture_Standards.pdf (Section 2.0) + SGS_Information_Security_Protocol.pdf (Section 5.0)

22. [HARD] Using the Employee Handbook, Sustainable Impact Charter, and Strategic Roadmap, describe SGS's investment in its people and culture.
    - ANSWER: $5,000 annual learning stipend, 16 weeks paid parental leave, 401(k) 4% match (Employee Handbook, Section 2.0). 24 hours paid volunteer time, $2,000 donation matching (Sustainable Impact, Section 3.0). 40% women/non-binary workforce target (Sustainable Impact, Section 2.0). Hiring across 3 new geographic regions (Strategic Roadmap, Section 1.0).
    - SOURCE: SGS_Employee_Excellence_Handbook.pdf (Section 2.0) + SGS_Sustainable_Impact_Charter.pdf (Sections 2.0, 3.0) + SGS_Strategic_Roadmap_2026.pdf (Section 1.0)

23. [VERY HARD] Across the Quality Audit Framework, Technical Debt Guidelines, and Cloud Architecture Standards, trace the full lifecycle of a code change from review to production.
    - ANSWER: Two reviewer approvals incl. one Senior Engineer (Quality Audit, Section 4.0). CI/CD pipeline: lint, test, build, security scan, staging, manual approval, blue-green deploy (Cloud Arch, Section 4.0). 80% backend test coverage required (Quality Audit, Section 1.0). 5-gate release sign-off process (Quality Audit, Section 3.0). 72-hour post-release monitoring (Quality Audit, Section 5.0). 20% sprint time reserved for tech debt (Tech Debt, Section 2.0).
    - SOURCE: SGS_Quality_Audit_Framework.pdf (Sections 1.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0) + SGS_Cloud_Architecture_Standards.pdf (Section 4.0) + SGS_Technical_Debt_Guidelines.pdf (Section 2.0)

24. [HARD] Using the Data Sovereignty Policy, Information Security Protocol, and Vendor Compliance Manual, what happens when a data breach occurs involving a third-party vendor?
    - ANSWER: Internal escalation to CISO within 1 hour (Data Sovereignty, Section 5.0). GDPR breach notification to supervisory authority within 72 hours (Data Sovereignty, Section 5.0). Vendor system access revoked within 24 hours (Vendor Manual, Section 5.0). Incident classified as SEV-1 or SEV-2 based on scope (InfoSec, Section 5.0). Vendor undergoes formal review and potential contract termination (Vendor Manual, Section 4.0).
    - SOURCE: SGS_Data_Sovereignty_Policy.pdf (Section 5.0) + SGS_Information_Security_Protocol.pdf (Section 5.0) + SGS_Vendor_Compliance_Manual.pdf (Sections 4.0, 5.0)

25. [HARD] Across the Strategic Roadmap, Sustainable Impact Charter, and Data Sovereignty Policy, what are SGS's key geographic and compliance considerations for global expansion?
    - ANSWER: Expanding into Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, and Latin America (Strategic Roadmap, Section 1.0). Data must reside in customer's geographic region with centers in US, EU, and APAC (Data Sovereignty, Section 1.0). GDPR compliance with 30-day DSAR fulfillment for EU customers (Data Sovereignty, Section 2.0). Net-zero carbon by 2030 across all locations (Sustainable Impact, Section 1.0). Platinum-tier cloud partnerships with AWS, Azure, Google Cloud (Strategic Roadmap, Section 4.0).
    - SOURCE: SGS_Strategic_Roadmap_2026.pdf (Sections 1.0, 4.0) + SGS_Data_Sovereignty_Policy.pdf (Sections 1.0, 2.0) + SGS_Sustainable_Impact_Charter.pdf (Section 1.0)
